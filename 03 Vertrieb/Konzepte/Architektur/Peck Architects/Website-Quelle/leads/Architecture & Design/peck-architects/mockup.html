( about us )

William Peck, AIA

Bill began working in the architectural field in 1982 and founded Peck Architects in 1995 in Old Town Lewisville. Even before becoming an architect, Bill was a trim carpenter and construction manager.

He incorporates the latest in conventional and renewable building technology to design truly custom projects that reflect each client’s style and needs, including accessibility. In the Old Town Lewisville Historic District, his firm completed the revitalization of seventeen Main Street projects.
