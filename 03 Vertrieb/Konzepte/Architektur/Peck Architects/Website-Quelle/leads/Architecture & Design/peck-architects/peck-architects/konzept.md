# Konzept: Peck Architects (Lewisville, Texas)

Stand: 05.10.2026. Private Konzeptvorschau von Northframe, nicht öffentlich.

## Designrichtung
Many varied projects in Old Town Lewisville and beyond: a horizontal portfolio of 20 named projects suits it, deco type fits the restored storefronts.

**Inspiration:** TARQ Studio (letters textured by a photo, horizontal project track, bracket captions, vertical side counter).

**Signature:** The word PECK is cut out of a project photo that pans through the letters. Portfolio is a pinned horizontal track with bracket captions and a 01/20 side counter.

## Inhalt (echt vs. Platzhalter)
- Echt von peckarchitects.com: Twenty named projects with categories, William Peck bio and awards, Maria Watts and Jessica Bowling, five services, address, phone, mail.
- Offen: Category of Alcorn Office Building is [placeholder]. Unnamed images (126 Church, 940 Bar, Blackmon, 320 W Main) are left out until the firm names them.

## Technik
Statisches HTML/CSS/JS, GSAP und ScrollTrigger aus `../../_presentation/vendor/` (nur wo animiert), Schriften über Google Fonts (OFL). Vorschau: `leads-static` (Port 5180), Pfad `Architecture & Design/peck-architects/mockup.html`. Reduced-motion und Mobil (390 px) sind berücksichtigt.
