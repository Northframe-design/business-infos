# Konzept: Magee Architects (Fort Worth, Texas)

Stand: 05.10.2026. Private Konzeptvorschau von Northframe, nicht öffentlich.

## Designrichtung
Forty years of civic work in eight categories and 20+ real project photos per category: the index is the design.

**Inspiration:** Driessen Architectuur (numbered sections, a large index) and TARQ Studio (type-led black stage).

**Signature:** Eight numbered categories open like drawers into a grid of real project photos with names.

## Inhalt (echt vs. Platzhalter)
- Echt von magee-architects.com: Project names from the category lists, 44 thumbnails matched to names by the source file order, services and eight phases, about text, contact.
- Offen: Recreation shows photos only for 11 of 16 names (the others are listed without image). Cover photos of the small categories are not tied to a single project. Street address is [placeholder] (the site shows only city and ZIP).

## Technik
Statisches HTML/CSS/JS, GSAP und ScrollTrigger aus `../../_presentation/vendor/` (nur wo animiert), Schriften über Google Fonts (OFL). Vorschau: `leads-static` (Port 5180), Pfad `Architecture & Design/magee-architects/mockup.html`. Reduced-motion und Mobil (390 px) sind berücksichtigt.
