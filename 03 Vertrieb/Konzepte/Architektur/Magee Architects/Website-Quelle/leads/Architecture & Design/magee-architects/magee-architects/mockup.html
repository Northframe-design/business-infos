( about us )

A full service architectural firm in Fort Worth, Texas.

Magee Architects’ design and project management philosophy is very simple: provide the client quality building design and great service through strong team dynamics. Our reputation for quality design and remarkable service to our clients has been established through commissions over the past 40+ years.

Good communication and teamwork are essential in all phases of the project. Attention to design detail and client needs have been the key to repeat commissions and the success of our firm.

Quality design
Buildings that are secure, efficient, long lasting, technologically up to date, and adaptable to future expansion needs.
Environment
Form and plan are related to the site, the region, and the climate, so the building enhances the site.
Energy
Designed to minimize the cost of ownership and the building’s impact on the environment over its life span.
