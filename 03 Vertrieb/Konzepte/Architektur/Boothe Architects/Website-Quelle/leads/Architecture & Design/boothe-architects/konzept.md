# Konzept: Boothe Architects (Fort Worth, Texas)

Stand: 05.10.2026. Private Konzeptvorschau von Northframe, nicht öffentlich.

## Designrichtung
A long-standing Near Southside firm with a blue wordmark: paper-white blueprint look, real project gallery in the middle.

**Inspiration:** M3 Planungsgruppe (logo mask that turns solid and shrinks into the header) and Driessen Architectuur (calm numbered structure, kept as small labels).

**Signature:** The logo mask shows four project photos, turns solid blue and settles into the header. The gallery filters by the six categories of the current site.

## Inhalt (echt vs. Platzhalter)
- Echt von boothearchitects.com: Sixteen named projects with their categories, Ray W. Boothe and George S. Kriske (bios, mails, phone), services, history (founded 1985 commitment, practice since 1983, 1979 passive solar award).
- Offen: Street address, details of two service blocks and the firm contact data besides Ray Boothe are [placeholder]. The current site shows 2020 in the copyright line; confirm with the firm.

## Technik
Statisches HTML/CSS/JS, GSAP und ScrollTrigger aus `../../_presentation/vendor/` (nur wo animiert), Schriften über Google Fonts (OFL). Vorschau: `leads-static` (Port 5180), Pfad `Architecture & Design/boothe-architects/mockup.html`. Reduced-motion und Mobil (390 px) sind berücksichtigt.
