About us

Fort Worth’s full-service architectural firm. Creativity in progress.

In 1985 the founding principal of Boothe Architects, Inc., Ray W. Boothe, made the commitment to forward the idea of “creativity in progress” in all clients’ projects. The firm is known for its brilliant utilization of creativity to encompass conceptual design, site planning, adaptive use, interior architecture and optimization of client usage.

We are the oldest architecture firm in the Near Southside area. Our local roots give us the perspective and attitude of a smaller firm, but our resources and experience allow us to tackle challenges of nearly any size, spanning the healthcare, commercial, residential, and education sectors.
