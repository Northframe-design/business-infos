# Konzept: conduit architecture + design (McKinney, Texas)

Stand: 05.10.2026. Private Konzeptvorschau von Northframe, nicht öffentlich.

## Designrichtung
Thin lowercase logo plus strong civic photography: the logo itself is the hero, the photos do the talking.

**Inspiration:** M3 Planungsgruppe (logo as a window onto the work, then solid, then into the header) and TARQ Studio (horizontal project track, bracket captions).

**Signature:** The logo is a window: seven project photos run through it, it turns solid white and settles into the header while scrolling. Projects sit in a pinned horizontal track with a 01/07 counter on desktop.

## Inhalt (echt vs. Platzhalter)
- Echt von conduitad.com: Seven named projects (Van Alstyne Public Safety, Anna Fire Station No. 2, McKinney Fire Station No. 11, TUPPS Brewery, Legend Bank, TPD Smiles Orthodontics, Shops at the Gate), principals Kobey Seale and Eric Ciskowski with their quotes, Caitlin Dettling (joined 2017), address, phone and mail, founded 2013, project types.
- Offen: No placeholders in use. Project locations (Sherman, Frisco) come from the source site captions and should be re-checked before sending.

## Technik
Statisches HTML/CSS/JS, GSAP und ScrollTrigger aus `../../_presentation/vendor/` (nur wo animiert), Schriften über Google Fonts (OFL). Vorschau: `leads-static` (Port 5180), Pfad `Architecture & Design/conduit-architecture/mockup.html`. Reduced-motion und Mobil (390 px) sind berücksichtigt.
