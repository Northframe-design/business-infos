# Konzept: Laurie Murphy Architect (Fort Worth, Texas)

Stand: 05.10.2026. Private Konzeptvorschau von Northframe, nicht öffentlich.

## Designrichtung
Few projects but seven clear categories and a strong logo with a skyline: a light, calm page with one large photo that switches per category.

**Inspiration:** Illoca (calm, centered mark and a line that draws itself, here the ground line under the hero) and Driessen Architectuur (one large image instead of a thumbnail grid).

**Signature:** The work list swaps one large photo (hover, click or keyboard focus). A ground line draws under the hero like the base of the skyline in the logo.

## Inhalt (echt vs. Platzhalter)
- Echt von lmurphyarchitect.com: Seven work categories with their real photos, three reasons to choose, services, project types, phone, mail, area. Claims such as "21 years" are quoted from the current site.
- Offen: Resume page is not reproduced (images of a resume). No street address on the source site, none shown.

## Technik
Statisches HTML/CSS/JS, GSAP und ScrollTrigger aus `../../_presentation/vendor/` (nur wo animiert), Schriften über Google Fonts (OFL). Vorschau: `leads-static` (Port 5180), Pfad `Architecture & Design/laurie-murphy-architect/mockup.html`. Reduced-motion und Mobil (390 px) sind berücksichtigt.
