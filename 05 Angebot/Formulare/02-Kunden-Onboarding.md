# 02 — Kunden-Onboarding / Website onboarding

Einsatz: nach Auftragserteilung. In drei übersichtlichen Abschnitten anzeigen. Englische Kundentexte; interne Hinweise nicht veröffentlichen. Textentwurf, noch kein technisch verbundenes Formular.

## Überschrift

Let's build your website.

## Einleitung

Share the details we need for your first draft. Short answers are fine. If something isn't ready, let us know — we'll flag what's missing before the site goes live.

Fields marked * are required. Only information marked for your website is intended for publication.

## Abschnitt 2: Your content

Your package includes Home, Services, About, Reviews/Projects, and Contact. We'll shape your answers into a clear first draft.

| Label | Typ | Pflicht | Hilfetext / Auswahl |
|---|---|---|---|
| Main services | Mehrzeiliger Text | Ja | List up to five main services. Put the most important one first. |
| About your business | Mehrzeiliger Text | Nein | A few facts about your team, approach, and story. Only include dates and experience you can confirm. |
| What should visitors do first? | Einzelauswahl | Ja | Call us / Request an estimate through the form |
| Logo and photo folder link | URL | Nein | Share a folder containing your logo and approved business or project photos. Give us viewing/download access only. Please exclude private customer information. |
| What is still missing? | Mehrfachauswahl | Nein | Logo / Photos / Business description / Reviews or project details / Nothing |
| Project details | Mehrzeiliger Text | Nein | Match each photo filename to the work shown and its general location. Don't include a customer's full address. |
| Review sources | URL oder mehrzeiliger Text | Nein | Link to your Google Business Profile or other original review sources. We'll confirm what can be used before publishing. |
| Licenses, certifications, or warranties to mention | Mehrzeiliger Text | Nein | Include supporting links or documents and any limits or conditions. Leave blank if not applicable. |
| Style preferences | Mehrzeiliger Text | Nein | Share brand colors, one or two website examples, or anything you'd like us to avoid. |

## Abschnitt 3: Setup and review

| Label | Typ | Pflicht | Hilfetext / Auswahl |
|---|---|---|---|
| Current website | URL | Nein | Leave blank if this is your first website. |
| Domain status | Einzelauswahl | Ja | I own a domain / I need a domain / I'm not sure |
| Domain name | Text | Bedingt | Required if you own a domain. For example: example.com. |
| Domain provider | Text | Nein | Where is your domain managed? For example: GoDaddy or Cloudflare. “Not sure” is fine. |
| Does your business email use this domain? | Einzelauswahl | Bedingt | If you own a domain: Yes / No / I'm not sure |
| Email provider | Text | Nein | If known. This helps us preserve your existing business email during setup. |
| Who approves the website? | Text | Ja | Name one person who will collect feedback and approve the draft. |
| Approval contact email | E-Mail | Bedingt | Only if different from the project contact email. |
| Preferred launch date | Datum | Nein | This is a preference, not a confirmed launch date. We'll confirm timing once we've reviewed the materials. |
| Anything else we should know? | Mehrzeiliger Text | Nein | Tell us about any existing features or business details that must be preserved. Requests outside your package need a separate scope agreement. |

Never share passwords, payment details, recovery codes, or customer records here. If account access is needed, we'll arrange an invitation separately.

### Bestätigung vor Absenden

Pflicht-Checkbox, nicht vorausgewählt:

I've checked the information above and am authorized to share these materials for this website project. I've flagged anything that still needs confirmation.

### Erwartungsmanagement

Your package includes one revision round. When the draft is ready, please collect your team's feedback into one list. We'll ask the named approver to review the final version before publication.
